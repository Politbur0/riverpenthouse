The Space

A spacious penthouse
overlooking the river.
Spectacular sunsets.

Hilltop's River Penthouse occupies the entire top floor of a Main Street building in Narrowsburg, NY — perched directly above the deepest stretch of the Delaware River. With west-facing floor-to-ceiling windows, the setting sun creates a nightly spectacle that never gets old.

Inside, no detail was overlooked. High-end finishes, hotel-quality bedding, and décor curated to the level of a design magazine. This is not a typical vacation rental — it's the nicest rental in Upstate New York.
