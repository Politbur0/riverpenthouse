The Town

A world away.
90 miles from NYC.

Perched above the deepest stretch of the Delaware River, Narrowsburg sits at the edge of the western Catskills in Sullivan County, NY. A lively, walkable Main Street town with a vibrant art scene — the kind of place that's been quietly drawing artists, chefs, and weekenders from New York City for decades.

From the Penthouse, almost everything is within a two-minute walk. The restaurants below are the best of what Narrowsburg has to offer — all within easy reach.

Right on the River

The classic
Narrowsburg day trip.

Lander's River Trips has been launching rafts, canoes, kayaks, and tubes onto the Delaware River for decades — a Narrowsburg institution. Five launch and landing points along the river let you pick your own level of adventure: a 2.5-hour float, a full 5-hour ride, or an overnight camping trip at Skinners Falls.

First-time river-goers, families with kids, and experienced paddlers alike. Rafts hold 4 to 6 people — ideal for groups. The Delaware here is a leisurely scenic float, not whitewater — gorgeous scenery suitable for all ages.

Book ahead in summer — weekends fill up fast.

On the Water
Delaware River Rafting & Kayaking

Landers River Trips offers rafting, kayaking, and scenic floats on the Delaware River. A leisurely float rather than whitewater — gorgeous scenery and suitable for all ages. An absolute must in the warmer months. Departing from Narrowsburg, NY.

📍 Short walk from the Penthouse
Wildlife
Bald Eagle Watching on the Delaware

The Delaware River corridor near Narrowsburg is one of the premier bald eagle habitats in New York State. Watch them fish the river from the Penthouse windows every morning, or head to the riverbank for a closer look. Best in winter and early spring.

📍 From your window

Galleries & Narrowsburg Main Street

Narrowsburg has developed a vibrant art scene — independent galleries, studios, and boutiques lining Main Street. The Narrowsburg Union arts center hosts rotating exhibitions and events year-round in Sullivan County.

Bethel Woods
Center for the Arts.

Bethel Woods sits on the legendary grounds of the 1969 Woodstock Music Festival — just 30 minutes from Narrowsburg. One of the most iconic outdoor concert venues in America, with a full summer and fall season of major acts in Bethel, NY.

When a big show comes to town, the Penthouse is the perfect basecamp. Skip the overpriced hotels, wake up the next morning to bald eagles and river views, and have The Heron downstairs for dinner before the show.

Getting to Narrowsburg NY

Two hours
from the city.

Approximately 90 miles from Midtown Manhattan — a straightforward 2 to 2.5 hour drive from New York City to Narrowsburg, NY. Free street parking directly outside the Penthouse on Main Street.

1
Take I-87 North (New York State Thruway) from Manhattan toward upstate New York.
2
Exit onto Route 17 West toward Monticello and the Sullivan County Catskills.
3
Follow signs toward Narrowsburg via Route 17 West to Route 97 North along the Delaware River.
4
Arrive on Main Street Narrowsburg, NY. The Penthouse entrance is at 44 Main Street with free street parking directly outside.
