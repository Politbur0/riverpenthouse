Where the
Penthouse belongs.

Hilltop's River Penthouse is the Narrowsburg outpost of the Hilltop Collection — a family of luxury private rental properties just five minutes away in Beach Lake, Pennsylvania. Hilltop Castle World sits on 130 private acres and accommodates groups of 20 to 92+ guests across four distinct properties: The Castle, The Mansion, The Farmhouse, and The Lodge.

The Penthouse works perfectly as overflow for larger gatherings — your extra guests stay right on the Delaware River in Narrowsburg while the main group takes over the Castle, Mansion, Farmhouse, or Lodge in Beach Lake, PA.
